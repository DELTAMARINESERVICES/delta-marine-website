ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Experience You Can Trust Since 1991

Delta Marine Services is a marine surveying and inspection service provider based in Mangalore, Karnataka, India.

Since 1991, we have been providing marine surveying, cargo supervision, petroleum inspection, vessel inspection and container inspection services.

Our professional services are intended to support ship owners, cargo interests, insurers, shipping companies and other maritime industry stakeholders.

We are associated with recognised professional organisations and hold the stated IRDA licence and professional memberships.
